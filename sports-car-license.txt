The navigation car is original geometry built in index.html.

It is a stylized sports compact: a lofted body and greenhouse, separate lamps, mirrors, and wheels. No third-party car mesh is embedded.

CC0 candidates were inspected and not used: the Kenney Car Kit sedan, and the Kiln generic hatchback (https://assets.kilnstudio.tools/packs/vehicles/r4-local-review/models/hatchback.glb, CC0-1.0). Both are low-poly faceted shells, not a premium compact.
